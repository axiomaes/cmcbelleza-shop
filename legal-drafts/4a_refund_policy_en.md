# RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

We want you to love your purchase from **[COMPANY_NAME]**. If you are not entirely satisfied, we are here to help in accordance with United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) customer guidelines.

## 1. 30-DAY RETURN WINDOW

You have **30 calendar days** to return an item from the date you received it. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and in its original packaging. 

## 2. NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS (HYGIENE EXCEPTIONS)

Due to health, safety, and hygiene regulations in the US, **certain types of beauty and personal care items cannot be returned** once opened or used:
*   Opened or tested skincare, cosmetics, or makeup products.
*   Hair accessories that have been unsealed.
*   Used personal care tools.
*   Gift cards.

*If a cosmetic product arrived defective, damaged, or caused an adverse physical reaction, please contact our support immediately for a replacement or specialized refund resolution.*

## 3. HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN

To initiate a return, please email **[EMAIL]** with:
1.  Your Order Number.
2.  A clear description of the reason for return.
3.  (For damaged items) High-quality photos of the product and packaging.

**Do not send the item back to the manufacturer or return address listed on your package before contacting us.** We operate via supplier networks and must issue you a specific Return Authorization Number (RMA) and the correct shipping address.

## 4. RETURN SHIPPING COSTS

*   You will be responsible for paying for your own shipping costs for returning your item. Shipping costs are non-refundable.
*   If the return is due to our error (damaged or incorrect item), we will cover return postage costs or provide a prepaid shipping label.

## 5. REFUNDS PROCESS

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will send you an email to notify you that we have received it. We will immediately notify you of the status of your refund.

If your return is approved, we will initiate a refund to your original credit card (or original method of payment). You will receive the credit within a certain amount of days, depending on your card issuer's policies (typically **5 to 10 business days**).
